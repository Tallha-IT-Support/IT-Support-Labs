# Day 6 — Week 1 Review — Mapping a Home Network to the OSI Model

## Key Concepts
Consolidation day applying Days 1-5 to a real network: documenting router,
devices, and connection types the way a technician would, layer by layer.

## Why It Matters
Being able to walk through your own network layer-by-layer is a common informal
L1 interview exercise ("walk me through what happens when you open a browser").

## Interview Q&A
**Q: Why is a bottom-up (physical-first) troubleshooting approach preferred?**
Physical issues are the most common root cause and fastest to rule out —
checking a cable takes seconds versus wasting an hour chasing the wrong layer.
